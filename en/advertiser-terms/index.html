Advertiser Terms

This is the concise baseline for BMP Terbuka advertising and sponsorship.

  1. Campaigns must be lawful and compatible with applicable platform rules.
  2. Advertisers are responsible for the accuracy, rights, and claims in submitted material.
  3. BMP Terbuka may reject or remove illegal, deceptive, harmful, malicious, fraudulent, or platform-incompatible material.
  4. No conversion, sales, leads, revenue, or business outcome is guaranteed.
  5. Reach/impression/click/dismiss figures use BMP Terbuka's applicable measurement methodology.
  6. Advertising does not provide access to personal user identity, Telegram/private activation identity, document/OCR/PDF content, or private per-user telemetry.
  7. Advertising does not grant product-control rights.
  8. Payment, cancellation, and make-good terms follow the specific campaign agreement/order.
