# Funding & Transparency

BMP Terbuka dapat didukung melalui Supporter, sponsorship, advertising, partnership, dan potential investment.

## Batas governance

- Sponsor bukan product authority.
- Advertiser bukan akses ke user data.
- Investor bukan automatic operational control.
- Funding bukan kemampuan untuk override privacy/security boundary.

Repository/website tidak boleh mengarang valuation, equity terms, investor rights, revenue-share, confidential revenue/cost, atau investment agreement yang belum ada. Arrangement nyata menggunakan agreement terpisah dan dipublikasikan hanya sejauh perlu/diizinkan.
