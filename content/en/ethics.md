# Ethics & Independence

BMP Terbuka separates funding from authority over users and the core product.

Sponsors, advertisers, partners, and investors do not automatically receive access to personal data or private per-user telemetry.

Paying parties cannot require weaker security controls, arbitrary executable tracking, changes to privacy boundaries, or control over core OCR behavior, moderation, access rules, or product integrity.

Material conflicts of interest should be disclosed proportionately.

Any real investment or partnership rights exist only through separate agreements; they are not implied by the website or repository.
