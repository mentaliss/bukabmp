# Funding & Transparency

BMP Terbuka may be supported through Supporter contributions, sponsorship, advertising, partnerships, and potential investment.

## Governance boundary

- Sponsor is not product authority.
- Advertiser is not user-data access.
- Investor is not automatic operational control.
- Funding does not override privacy or security boundaries.

The public project must not fabricate valuation, equity terms, investor rights, revenue share, confidential financials, or investment agreements that do not exist.
