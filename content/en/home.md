# Learning material you can access, made easier to study.

BMP Terbuka turns material you already have legitimate access to into searchable PDFs, with OCR and PDF assembly performed locally on your device.

**Open source · Local-first · Privacy-conscious**

## What BMP Terbuka is

A browser-extension learning workflow: open material through your normal access, choose the material/module range, run local OCR, save PDFs, and resume from local cache without repeating completed work.

## Privacy by design

Document pages, OCR text, and generated PDFs are not sent to the BMP Terbuka backend. Product telemetry is pseudonymous and current advertising is contextual/non-personalized.

## Business model

BMP Terbuka may be supported by Supporter contributions, sponsorship, advertising, partnerships, and potential investment. Funding does not buy user-data access or control over privacy, security, or the core product.
