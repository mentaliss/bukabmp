# Responsible Use

BMP Terbuka is intended to help users work with material they are already authorized to access.

Do not use it for unauthorized access, credential bypass, access-control circumvention, piracy, illegal redistribution, watermark removal, or attempts to disguise the source of material.

The extension uses the user's existing browser session and is designed to stop when the source denies access or requests re-authentication.

Access to material does not automatically grant redistribution or commercial-use rights. Users remain responsible for making sure their use of exported files is permitted.
