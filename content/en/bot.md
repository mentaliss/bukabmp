# Bot & Community

BMP Terbuka has an official Telegram channel, group, and bot.

Website documentation and [Status & Version](../status) are the primary product references.

## Channel

https://t.me/bukabmp

Used for releases, compatibility status, security notices, and important updates.

## Group Terbuka

https://t.me/+0pAg9ymEhWdkZmNl

**A community playground for chatting, helping each other, sharing, joining activities, discussing tools, learning, and more.**

Technical BMP Terbuka discussion is welcome, but it is only one activity in the community rather than the group's sole purpose.

## Bot

https://t.me/bukabmp_bot

Username: `@bukabmp_bot`

The bot supports activation, help menus, Supporter, Quiz, and AI support.

Use `/ask`, mention `@bukabmp_bot`, or reply to the bot when you want AI help in the group. The bot is not intended to answer every normal conversation.

AI support can help with installation, updates, activation, backup/restore, storage, PDFs, Telegram Quiz, and troubleshooting.

## Donation & Supporter

Support is optional. Use only the official BMP Terbuka/bot flow; do not send payment or credentials to unofficial accounts.

## Do Not Send

Do not send passwords, OTPs, cookies/sessions, activation tokens, pairing secrets, API keys, backup files, or learning material you are not allowed to redistribute.
