# Bot & Community

BMP Terbuka uses Telegram channels, a group, and a bot for updates, activation, discussion, and support.

The website documentation and [Status & Version](../status) are the primary product references. The bot helps users find information and performs specific functions, but it is not a replacement for the official docs.

- important BMP Terbuka updates.

## Asking the Bot in the Group

The bot is not intended to answer every group conversation.

To call the bot in Group Terbuka, use one of these:

- type `/ask` followed by your question;
- mention `@bukabmp_bot`;
- reply to a bot message and write your question.

Normal group conversation is not intended to trigger AI.

## Example Questions

Example:

`/ask how do I install on Android?`

or mention the bot:

`@bukabmp_bot failed to fetch when I press Start`

For error questions, include the stage where the error appears.

## Bot in Direct Messages

AI access in bot DMs may differ by account/entitlement.

Supporter Pass includes bot DM + unlimited AI while the Supporter entitlement is active. Authorized special accounts may also have DM access.

Regular users use the AI path provided in Group Terbuka under the current product rules.

## AI Support

AI support is focused on BMP Terbuka, not general-purpose questions.

Appropriate topics include:

- installation;
- activation;
- updates;
- BMP Code;
- module processing;
- PDFs;
- storage;
- errors;
- Supporter Pass.

AI can misunderstand a question. If an AI answer conflicts with the website documentation or Status page, **the documentation/Status page is the authority**.

## AI Quota

Regular AI access can be limited.

The current bot configuration allows up to **6 AI questions per day** on the regular path.

Active Supporters receive unlimited AI while their Supporter entitlement remains active.

That limit applies to the AI product path, not to ordinary community messages.

## Troubleshooting Context

The bot may use limited conversation context for follow-up troubleshooting.

Supporters receive a longer troubleshooting context window than the regular path.

Do not treat bot context as a permanent conversation archive.

## Reporting a Problem

If the bot does not solve the issue, send a report that is easy to inspect.

Include:

- screenshot;
- BMP Terbuka version;
- Android or Desktop;
- browser;
- stage where the issue appears;
- exact error text;
- BMP Code/module range when relevant.

Clear stage information helps distinguish activation, reader, module-processing, and export problems.

## Data You Should Not Send

Do not send to the bot, group, or admins:

- passwords;
- OTPs;
- cookies;
- sessions;
- activation tokens;
- pairing secrets;
- API keys;
- other credentials;
- student IDs unless genuinely required.

If sharing a screenshot, make sure sensitive information is not visible.

## Activation Through the Bot

Activation starts from the extension. Do not invent an activation code directly in Telegram.

Open [Activation & Verification](activation) for the complete flow.
