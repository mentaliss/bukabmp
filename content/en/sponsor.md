# Sponsorship & Advertising

BMP Terbuka is open to lawful advertising, sponsorship, campaigns, and partnerships.

Campaigns may be considered when they are lawful, non-deceptive, non-malicious, and compatible with applicable platform/store rules and product-security requirements.

Obvious rejections include illegal gambling, scams, phishing, malware, illegal narcotics/drugs, pornography or sexually explicit advertising, illegal goods/services, and clearly fraudulent or materially deceptive campaigns.

Lawful but regulated categories are considered case by case.

## Available inventory

Potential formats include sponsor cards, interstitials, and broader campaigns/partnerships. Public pricing has not been finalized: **contact for pricing**.

A first conversation only needs the brand/product, campaign idea, target URL, campaign period, and budget.

## Privacy boundary

Advertising money does not buy access to personal identity, Telegram/private activation identity, document identity, per-user usage history, private telemetry, or arbitrary tracking JavaScript.

Advertising also does not grant control over privacy, security, core OCR behavior, moderation, user-access rules, or product integrity.
